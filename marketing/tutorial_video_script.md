## Production Notes for Screen Capture
1. **Demo Page**: Use `dist/demo/index.html` or equivalent neutral portal.
2. **Cursor Motion**: Smooth, deliberate cursor pacing (recommend using 60fps recording with mouse smoothing enabled).
3. **Soundtrack**: Subtle, upbeat electronic lo-fi background music ducked at -18dB under the voiceover.
4. **Color Grading**: High contrast dark-mode aesthetic to highlight the indigo and emerald UI accents.
