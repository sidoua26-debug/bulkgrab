How Freemium Licensing Works

  1. Step 1: NOWPayments Crypto Checkout
    In the extension popup, click "🪙 Pay with Crypto — $9.99". This opens the secure NOWPayments invoice to pay with USDT (BSC) or any supported cryptocurrency.
  2. Step 2: Retrieve Payment ID
    After completing payment, copy your Payment ID (e.g. 5829104812) from your confirmation screen or email receipt.
  3. Step 3: Activate
    Paste your Payment ID into the field above to activate Pro tier. (Note: Blockchain confirmations typically take 2–5 minutes).
